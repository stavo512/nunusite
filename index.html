philosophy

They say that the best design is often no design and it is certainly true that many iconic products arise from form following function: the circumstances in which and the problems for which they were made. Still, there is maybe still just enough wiggle-room for use to add a joke, a methaphor or some other small human touch that goes beyond the basic functionality of the design.
